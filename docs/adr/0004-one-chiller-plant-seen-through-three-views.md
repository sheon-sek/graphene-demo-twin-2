# One chiller plant with four chillers, seen through three views

The export describes the cooling plant three times, and the counts disagree. The UDT equipment has 3 chillers (R_C1–3) and 20 tower cells. `Chiller System Control` (the supervisory view) has 4 chillers (CH-001–004) and 4 towers. `Chiller_System` (the instrument view) has 4 chillers plus header, bypass and per-hall branch instruments. We model **one** physical plant with **four** chillers. R_C1–3 are CH-001–003, and CH-004 is an Unexported Asset, observed only through the two Plant Views. Each of CT-001–004 is a Tower Group of five UDT tower cells, dedicated to one chiller. This is the only reading under which the equipment counts reconcile: 9 chiller pumps = 4 × 2 + 1, and 16 chiller valves = 4 × 2 isolation valves + 4 header valves + 4 bypass valves. The Asset Model stays untouched, and a reader who expects the UDT list to be the full equipment inventory should know it is not.
